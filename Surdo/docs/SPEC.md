# Surdo: spec and state

A modal surdo (a samba bass drum, one drum per instance) for Max for Live, forked from Bongo (same panel, same engine structure, same fit pipeline) and fitted to the Latin Percussion pack's surdo takes. Usage: `../README.md`.

## What the recordings show
Sources (`tests/refs.py`): four one-shots, `Previews/Drums/Drum Hits/Percussion/Surdo {Open, Mute, Rim, Finger}.adg.ogg` (44.1 kHz stereo, 1.5-2.0 s, peak-normalised, one take each; the fit and plots use the mono mix), and seven clips, `Previews/Clips/{Samba 1, Samba 2, Baion B, Candombe B, Murga B, Rhumba Funk B, Afoxe Tambourin &} Surdo-*.alc.ogg` (5.8-10.7 s loops).

- **The clips replay the four one-shots.** `tests/clips.py stats`: matching the first 62 ms of each one-shot against each clip (normalised correlation > 0.97; > 0.9 for the Open, an 86 Hz tone that any neighbouring hit disturbs) finds 61 Open (onsets only: an Open match counts where the 3 ms envelope rises 6 dB, since later stretches of the same ring match too; 134 raw), 29 Mute and 16 Finger replays and no Rim. So the clips add no new stroke and no second drum: they give tuning, the Open's glide against level, and the strokes' loudness against each other in use. The strongest low peak of a clip hit is at 83-89 Hz (Open-like), 130-145 Hz (Mute-like) or below 65 Hz (Finger-like); there is no hit of another pitch, so **no size menu**: Tune +-12 st stands for the 1a / 2a / 3a sizes (the sizes themselves are not measured).
- **Level of the strokes against each other** (replay gain of the one-shot, whose peak is 1.0): Open median 0.80 (-2.0 dB, max 0.98), Mute 0.46 (-6.7 dB), Finger 0.37 (-8.7 dB). Used for `PEAK_DB` (Open -4, Mute -8.7, Finger -10.7 dBFS at velocity 1, i.e. 0 / -4.7 / -6.7 dB relative to the Open); the Rim is not in the clips: -5 dBFS is a judgement.
- **Tuning.** The Open's strong tone: 88.2 Hz at the hit, 86.0 Hz from 250 ms on; the 50 clean replays (no other hit within 0.32 s) settle at a median 85.98 Hz (80.2-91.2: the spread is neighbours). `F1 = 86.0` is the settled tone.
- **Pitch glide of the Open** (`measure.pitch`, a 70-105 Hz band-pass, Hilbert phase over 40 ms windows): 87.9 Hz (30 ms), 87.4 (90), 86.7 (150), 86.3 (210), 86.0 (300), flat after: about -43 ct, a decay with tau ~0.1 s, smooth, no wobble. Level dependence from the clean clip replays (f 40-100 ms against f 200-290 ms): replay gain 0.35-0.6: median 17 ct (n 11), over 0.6: 36 ct (n 37) (the one-shot itself 37 ct). Modelled as `gl x velocity^2` with tau 0.1 s (`gl` fitted so that at velocity 0.8 the glide is the one-shot's 1.95 %); that is steeper than linear in level, but a threshold would fit the three bins better; weak evidence. The Rim's 73 Hz tone does not glide (72.1 -> 73.4 Hz over 0.5 s); the Mute and Finger have no line clean enough to track.
- **Open decay**: not an exponential. The 86 Hz band envelope falls -23 dB/s for the first 250 ms (T60 2.6 s), -55 dB/s from 250 to 500 ms, -65..-76 dB/s after 450 ms (T60 0.8-0.9 s); levels -8 / -12 / -18 / -30 dB at 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.6 s; broadband t20 426 ms, t40 715 ms (then a -50 dB room floor). No beating: the 86 Hz envelope's ripple around a cubic is 0.23 dB rms, and the matrix pencil's "86 Hz, T60 1.8 s" + "87 Hz, T60 0.67 s" pair is its way of fitting the curved envelope, not a head-coupling doublet (a doublet this close would beat slower than the ring). Modelled by the stroke's G below 1 with tau 0.4 s: the decay rate starts at G x its late value and returns to it (`tests/fit.py` grid now reaches G = 0.25).
- **Modes** (peaks of the stroke spectra, 0.1-0.4 s windows, plus `tests/modal.py`; the four strokes ring different members of one set, at the same absolute frequencies: 159 and 237-259 Hz appear in the Open, Rim and Finger alike). Ratios to f1 = 86 Hz: the Finger's thump 0.30 / 0.42 / 0.54 (26, 36, 46 Hz, T60 about 0.12-0.25 s), 0.74 (64), the Rim's and Mute's **0.85 (73 Hz, T60 0.74-0.87 s on the Rim)**, **1.00 (86 Hz, the Open)**, 1.20 (103; Finger 102-106), 1.45 (125) and 1.65 (142; Mute 123-146), **1.85 (159 Hz, T60 about 1.1 s)**, 2.4 (206), 2.76 (237), **3.0 (259 Hz, T60 1.3 s)**, 3.83 (329), 4.4 (378), **5.2 (447 Hz, T60 1.3 s on the Rim)**, 5.86 (504). Above about 700 Hz the Rim's spectrum is a forest of lines at 0.8-1.4 kHz (T60 0.1-0.35 s) that is noise-like (flatness -2.7 dB at 1-2.5 kHz): modelled as noise bands. A ~35 Hz line sits in every take at -50..-70 dB from 0.1 s to 0.4 s (room or shell): not modelled, and the fit's ring terms skip modes under 50 Hz (`FMIN`).
- **Strokes**: Open (mallet, centre: 86 Hz, soft attack, nothing above 1 kHz), Mute (hand on the head: a thud, 20-250 Hz, -20 dB in 23 ms, -40 dB in 116 ms, a sharp first ms with a peak of 0.62), Rim (stick on the rim: 2-4 kHz at -5 dB re total, 73 Hz tone ringing 0.8 s, t20 75 ms, t40 290 ms), Finger (a ~25 Hz thump in the first 40 ms - peak 0.13 of the file, about half its energy under 32 Hz - then 100 Hz; slow attack, peak at 12 ms; t20 18 ms, t40 103 ms).
- **High frequencies: noise or lines** (spectral flatness 2.5-9 kHz, 5-40 ms): Mute -5.6 dB, Rim -5.0 dB, Finger -2.8 dB: noise (white = 0, a sinusoid -13..-25). The Open reads -14.8 dB, i.e. a tonal skirt, but that band holds -45 dB (2-4 kHz) and -61 dB (4-8 kHz) re the total, so it is nearly empty. Noise bands decay with T60 0.17-0.35 s (10-80 ms slopes), about 0.2 s above 2 kHz in every stroke; `BANDS`.
- **Contact tick** (first 3 ms, peak of the file): Mute 0.62, Rim 0.72, Finger 0.12, Open 0.05: the Open has no tick (a soft mallet); `tests/fit.py` fits a tick only where the recording has one (blip > 0.12) and otherwise matches the crack share.

## Model (scripts/surdo_engine.py)
Per hit: 17 head resonators at the ratios above of f1 (Tune, mod-wheel Press, glide), 7 noise bands (800 .. 7800 Hz). Contact pulse -> two one-pole low-passes (`fc`, Touch, velocity) -> modes with per-stroke gains W, a band-passed noise tick (`nl nf nq nt`); at the hit the ring on the head is multiplied by `kp` and every mode's decay rate by `1 + (G - 1) c(t)`, c falling from 1 with time constant `tau` (a soft hit adds `vel` x the missing part of c, so a quiet Open over a ringing Open does not slow its ring again). Skin tilts the mode gains (+-6 dB/oct of ratio to f1), Vary jitters each sinusoid's gain by up to 1.5 dB std at every hit. Tables `scripts/surdo_tables.json` come from `tests/fit.py` and `tests/calibrate.py`. Differences from Bongo: one drum (no Drum menu), 4 strokes (no Flam), no 125 Hz body or Shell control (there is no measured fixed-Hz body tone here), glide, G < 1, early window 120 ms and ring window 0.2-0.7 s, hits 2 s apart in every render, and the noise bands may share the stroke's G.

## Surdo Musical (`m4l/SurdoMusical.amxd`)
A build-time flag, not a second engine: `surdo_engine.code(musical=True)` / `build_voice.py --musical` (the plain Surdo's generated `.maxpat`, `surdo_midi.js` and `.amxd` are byte-identical to before). Differences:
- **Pitch.** One more gen~ Param, `key` (default `KEY0` = 41), set by `surdo_musical_midi.js` ahead of `vel` and `trg` (so the hit plays at the new key). The control-rate line `vF1 = 86 * exp2(tune/12)` becomes `vF1 = mtof(key) * exp2(tune/12)`; everything downstream of `vF1` (the 17 head modes, the glide as a fraction of f1, Press) is untouched, so key and Tune add in semitones. `mtof` is exact 12-TET, A4 = 440 Hz: note 41 is 87.31 Hz (the recorded 86.0 Hz is 26 ct under it, not snapped).
- **Fixed-Hz parts stay fixed**, as under Tune: the 7 noise bands (800-7800 Hz), the contact filters and the tick; the head modes' T60s are in seconds, so low keys ring as long as the recorded drum and high keys do not shorten (a small drum would). The Skin tilt is by mode ratio, so it is unchanged.
- **Nyquist.** In the control block each head mode's `vW` is multiplied by `(w < 2 pi x 0.45)`: a mode whose rotation (including glide, Press and Tune) passes 0.45 x samplerate loses its gain until the next hit (the gen~ comparison is a first-compile-safe one: it lives in the History-derived control block). Key 110 (4.7 kHz) mutes the 5.86x mode only; key 127 + Tune +12 mutes 13 of 17 modes and leaves the noise bands. Nothing is done at the low end: the rotations are stable at any frequency (key 0 = 8.2 Hz is sub-audio but finite).
- **Tune** stays +-12 st (no reason to shrink it: it is the same path and the keys now cover the large moves, but the range lets one offset a whole part, or detune two instances).
- **Mono and retune.** A new note retunes the modes still ringing from the previous one at once (the ring keeps its phase, so no click) and the new stroke's `kp` damps it as before. With the Open (`kp` 0.9) that is a short portamento-like glide of the old ring into the new note.
- **Verified.** Node (`tests/test_musical.js`, 11): `vF1` equals mtof(key) x 2^(Tune/12) for all 128 keys at Tune -12 / 0 / 3.3 / 12; measured Open tone (0.5-1.2 s, Vary 0) within 5 ct of mtof for every key 28..60 (worst 0.07 ct; keys 16 .. 108 spot-checked, 0.2 ct); key 41 vs 53 = 1200.0 ct; Tune adds; nyquist muting; keys 0..127 x Tune +-12 x 4 strokes finite under full scale. Standalone Max cold: gen_compile clean, `tests/test_musical_max.py` 14 checks (keys 28, 41, 53, 60, 72 within 0.1 ct; extremes 0, 16, 110, 127 finite). Live: `scripts/live_check_musical.py` 11 checks. Not done: by ear, and the panel (shared with Surdo) shows no pitch.

## Traps hit (beyond the ones in Bongo's spec)
- Bongo's measuring windows (40 ms early, 0.5 ms / 8 ms envelopes) see under four cycles of an 86 Hz tone and cannot separate 73 / 86 / 103 Hz; `measure.py` now uses 120 ms and a 3 ms envelope for the rise, and the ring window sits at 0.2-0.7 s.
- The ring-window band-level term of the decay search was dominated by the 35 Hz room line (-38 dB re total in the Mute's ring window): modes under 50 Hz are left out (`FMIN`).
- G on the sinusoid modes only left the Finger and Rim tails at 190 and 450 ms (their noise bands decay 0.2-0.3 s at G = 1); letting the noise bands share G fixed the tails but made the Mute's crack 9 dB too bright, so the search tries both and the cost has a crack term.
- Restarting c = 1 at every hit made a very soft Open over a ringing Open slow the ring again (+6 dB 0.2 s later); c now moves towards 1 by the velocity.
- `pkill -f "tests/fit.py fit"` kills every agent's fit; kill by pid.
