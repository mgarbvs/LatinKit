# Pandeiro: spec and state

One Max for Live instrument with an **Instrument** menu: a **Pandeiro** (frame-drum head + jingles, the platinelas) or a **Tamborim** (small tight head, played with a stick, no jingles). For the Latin kit. The head is Bongo's modal head, forked and refitted; the jingles are Shaker's PhISEM engine, copied, with one fitted constant set. Usage: `../README.md`.

## What the recordings show (tests/refs.py, 12 LP previews + the Roll; 12 jingle-tambourine previews as a cross-check)
- **Pandeiro A** (one take): Thumb, Finger, Slap, Rim, Shake, Roll. **Tmbourim A / B** (two takes): Open, Mute, Finger. The previews are peak-normalised: only shapes are compared; levels (`PEAK_DB`) are a judgement. Take B of the tamborim is at the same pitch as A (cross-correlation scale 1.000, correlation 0.81).
- **Pandeiro head**: strongest pooled peaks at about 250 Hz (F1), a dense cluster of weak modes from 0.56 to 1.8 x F1 (the head is a skin on a ring, tuned loose: T60 0.2-0.3 s, one at 1.8 x 0.6 s), nothing but noise above ~2.5 kHz in the head strokes. **Tamborim head**: F1 450 Hz, modes at 0.65-3.2 x F1, T60 0.25-0.55 s (the Open's 2.1 x mode rings longest), noise bands decaying in 60-150 ms. Mode ratios and T60s are in `scripts/pandeiro_engine.py` (`HEAD`), measured from the pooled spectra and band-envelope slopes.
- **Jingles** (Pandeiro Shake and Roll): above ~4.5 kHz the recording is broadband noise grains: spectral flatness 6-14 kHz is -5.6..-6.5 dB (white noise = 0, lines < -10), so not a few ringing lines. Grain density 250 /s (Shake), 315 /s (Roll); the jingle-tambourine previews 138-160 /s. The 1/3-octave levels of 4-16 kHz are a broad hump peaking at 10-12.5 kHz (-30 -23 -15 -7 -2 -8 dB). The ringing-band test (late/early energy) finds 7.1, 11.2, 14.2 and 14.8 kHz as the least-decayed bands in 3-4 of 4 head-stroke clips; a +-1.5 % peak list does not (33 peaks in 3-16 kHz, one per 380 Hz), so the jingle model stays a noise grain engine with a broad resonant filter and the tried "three ringing lines" start was dropped (spectrum error 1.1 dB rms vs 0.2).
- **Every pandeiro head stroke carries the jingles**: their 6.5-16 kHz share of the first 40 ms is -3.5 (Finger) .. -7.1 dB (Rim) re the stroke, build-up 8-16 ms to the 8-15.5 kHz peak, t20 of that envelope 74-128 ms. On the tamborim that share is -16..-21 dB (room and noise, no jingles).
- **Roll** (Pandeiro): not a table stroke. Its 150-900 Hz envelope has one strong line at **18.2 Hz** (taps; 39 and 67 Hz are harmonics), its length is 1.15 s (5 ms envelope within 30 dB of its peak); its taps are quiet next to the jingles (150-900 Hz sits 28 dB, 1-4 kHz 26 dB, 7-15 kHz 9 dB under the peak). The Tamborim has no roll recording.
- **Stroke list**: Pandeiro Thumb, Finger, Slap, Rim, Shake (jingles alone) + Roll; Tamborim Open, Mute, Finger. The Tamborim Mute damps the head (shortest t20, 31 ms vs 104 Open).

## Model (scripts/pandeiro_engine.py)
- **Head** (as Bongo): 12 resonators at ratios of F1 (Tune, mod-wheel Press), a fixed 125 Hz body (Shell), 5 decaying band-passed noise bands (2.0, 2.8, 4.0, 5.8, 8.5 kHz), a contact pulse through two one-poles (Touch), a band-passed noise tick, and the hand damping what rings (`kp`, `G`, `tau`). Table rows per stroke: mode gains `W`, damping, tick, noise decay `ns`, level `A`. On the pandeiro the top two noise bands belong to the jingles (`JOWN`).
- **Jingles** (Shaker's PhISEM): energy -> collision rate -> decaying sound level on noise -> 4th-order high-pass + three band-passes. Constants in `scripts/pandeiro_jingle.json`, fitted by `tests/jingle_fit.js` (Nelder-Mead on the Node shim, two stages: spectrum, then time) to the Shake and Roll previews pooled: 1/3-octave 4-16 kHz 0.3 dB rms, Shake rise 24 ms (ref 28), t20 63 (62), t30 99 (107), grain fluctuation cvE 0.17 (0.18), cvL 0.17 (0.15). Every head hit energises them (per-row coupling `jg` level, `jk` build-up ms, `js` decay multiplier, fitted by `tests/fit.py`); the Shake stroke is only the jingle layer. The **Jingles** control x `JG[instrument]` (1 / 0) is their level: a tamborim has none, the control does nothing there. Their filter coefficients are computed in the control block, not per sample.
- **Roll**: the instrument's Finger row repeated `ROLL_N` = 20 times `ROLL_GAP` = 1/18.2 s apart (1.1 s), the jingles energised every tap with `ROLL_J` = 14.35 x their level; taps at `ROLL_REL` = 0.149 x the velocity (Pandeiro, fitted by `tests/fit.py roll` to the recording's band levels) and 0.6 (Tamborim: a judgement, no recording). A new stroke cancels the pending taps.
- **Stroke menu**: one fixed list (Thumb, Finger, Slap, Rim, Shake, Roll, Open, Mute), `MAP[instrument][entry]` = the table row it plays. A stroke the instrument lacks plays the nearest it has: Pandeiro Open -> Thumb, Mute -> Finger; Tamborim Thumb / Slap / Rim -> Open, Shake / Roll -> Finger (Roll repeats it). Why not a per-instrument list: Live stores a menu parameter by its index, so a list that changes length under Instrument would point a saved preset at a different (or missing) stroke; one fixed list round-trips exactly.

## Traps hit (beyond the ones in the brief)
- **First compile in a fresh Max**: no ternary / max / min / clamp on an expression that depends only on Params at the top level (everything derived from `stroke`, `inst`, `vel`, `jingles` lives inside the hit block). `gen_compile.py --cold` guards it. The cold start is slow under load (a first try timed out at 30 s twice with the machine at load 7; the third passed).
- `calibrate.py` scales the row's `A` and `jg` together, but the jingle share it leaves is not the fitted one (the jingle then reads 2-3 dB low against the recordings). `tests/fit.py jg` re-sets `jg` after calibrate, three rounds of both, and is the last step of the re-fit.
- A 20 ms level window for the Tamborim Roll test lands between taps 55 ms apart and reads -56 dB: the check uses 60 ms.
- The kit Roll's fade over its last taps (`fit_roll.py`) puts the Tamborim's 0.9 s taps ~4 dB under its early ones (-51 dB): the "still tapping" check is relative to the early taps (within 18 dB), not a fixed -50 dB floor.
- `nl` (the tick's noise level) does not move the pandeiro Finger / Rim "blip" (the tone / jingle onset sets it); those two blips stay at 2.2 x and 2.7 x the recordings'.
- The Roll's first tap: the recording has no louder first tap, so every tap, the first too, is `ROLL_REL` x the velocity (a Roll is much quieter than a Finger by design: the recording's taps are 19 dB under its jingles).
- The Pandeiro Roll's jingles do not decay between taps in our render, and end at 1.1 s then fall in 0.2 s; the recording's envelope falls slowly through 1.0-1.5 s (`docs/compare/roll.png`).

## Demo
`docs/demo.wav` is rendered by `tests/test_voice_max.py --demo` from two device instances in Max. Its groove is one bar (16 slots at 120 bpm) of the LP clip "Samba 2 Tambourim Pandeiro-120bpm", read from the clip's onsets (identical in all 4 bars): on the pandeiro, onsets heavy at 150-500 Hz are Thumb, jingle-only ones (0.96 of the energy above 6 kHz) Finger or Shake, crackly ones Slap, the soft in-between ones (level 0.2) Finger ghosts; on the tamborim, onsets heavy at 0.5-2.5 kHz are Open when strong and Mute when they have a jingle-like tail (the pandeiro under them). The clip is read, not copied; no Ableton audio is stored in the repository.
