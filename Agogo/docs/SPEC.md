# Agogo: spec and state

A modal agogo bell (one bell per instance, High or Low) for Max for Live, forked from Bongo (same pipeline: tests/refs.py, extract.py, fit.py, calibrate.py, compare.py, measure.py, test_voice.js, test_voice_max.py, scripts/live_check_voice.py, same 180 x 169 panel) and fitted to the Latin Percussion pack's agogo previews. Usage: `../README.md`.

## What the recordings show (tests/refs.py: 7 LP previews, `Drum Hits/Bell/Agogo {A High, A Low, B High, B Mid, B Low, E High, E Low}`)
One open stick hit each, peak-normalised, 1.5-2 s. Core Library `Bells Agogo Hi.aif` is the same bell as A High (identical peaks): a duplicate, not a 4th take. `Factory Packs 2/.../Bells A-Agogo *.aif` are unreadable by ffmpeg: ignored.
- **Three kits are three different instruments**, not takes of one: A High 1470 Hz (T60 ~1.4 s), A Low 1003 Hz (~0.5 s); B High 1168, Mid ~1155, Low 906; E High 1357, Low 1009. Kit A is the fitted table; B and E are only measured (`fit.py report`), never averaged in. The Tune dial (+-12 st) covers the others.
- **Modes are doublets** (the bell is not axisymmetric). A High: 1469.2 / 1479.4 Hz, the partner 11 dB down, both T60 ~1.4-1.6 s: the ring envelope ripples at 10.1 Hz (`measure.split_of`; 9.5 Hz measured by hand). A Low: 1002.3 Hz (T60 0.47 s) and a long-lived 1017 Hz partner that only shows once the fundamental has died (the strongest peak 0.3-0.8 s in is 1016.9 Hz in the recording; its ripple is too small to measure in 0.4 s: the 6 Hz floor of `split_of`). The second modes 2340 / 2382 Hz of A Low are a doublet too. The +-30 Hz "sidebands" of a coarse peak-pick are window leakage: the table comes from a narrow-band matrix pencil over ~1 s windows (`tests/extract.py`).
- **The highs are tonal lines, not noise**: 2.5-9 kHz spectral flatness -10..-23 dB at 5-40 ms in A, B and E (the drums of Timbales / Bongo: -3..-6). They die in 10-300 ms. So the model has sinusoid partials up to 11 kHz (12 per bell: the real lines are denser than 60 Hz, neighbours within 60 Hz are merged into one partial of equal 40 ms energy); only a ~1 ms stick tick is noise.
- **Sympathetic ringing** of the other bell is ~-45 dB (B High's tail holds B Mid's 1155 Hz line): skipped.
- **A wide-band matrix pencil (800 Hz bands, order 10) only fitted noise** (hundreds of 10-100 ms components above 5 kHz): not used. The low group (< 4.5 kHz) uses a narrow band per spectral cluster, order 3-5, 0.03-0.93 s, refined by variable-projection least squares; the high group uses each partial's own band envelope (T60 from its 25 dB decay, level from its first-40-ms energy: the regression's intercept over-read the click).

## Strokes: what the clips show (none of the "other" strokes found)
Looked for hand-muted hits and a "clack" (bells squeezed together / wood handle) in: LP clips `Afoxe Agogo-107bpm`, `Plena Agogo-100bpm`, `Samba 2 Agogo-120bpm`, `Samba 1 Triangle Agogo-120bpm`, `Rhumba Funk B Cowbell Agogo-166bpm`; Core Library loops `Agogo Bells 90 bpm`, `Agogo Atabaques 112 bpm` (onsets from the high-passed 5 ms envelope; per hit f0, level at +20-40 ms, the band's fall at +60 / +120 / +200 ms, centroid).
- The LP clips are **MIDI renders of the pack's own one-shots**: every hit of a bell is the same to within 0.5 dB (A Low: -10 / -17 / -26 dB at +60 / +120 / +200 ms in every one of ~15 hits per clip; f0 1003 / 1470 Hz exact to 1 Hz; Samba 2 906 / 1021 Hz, Rhumba 1001-1003 Hz plus a 728 Hz cowbell). No muted hit, no clack: only the Open one-shot, repeated.
- The Core Library loops hold real playing, but with different bells (1358 Hz = E High, 1200, 1393, 956, 1760, 1970 Hz) and, in `Atabaques`, drums mixed in. In `Bells 90` the hits spaced 0.16 s are cut by the next hit (sharp vertical edges in the spectrogram: a sampler choke or an edit, not a hand); hits with a gap of 0.25 s or more ring until the next hit and fall -27..-39 dB by +200 ms, which is the small bell's own T60 (~0.35 s). No hit with a short ring against others of the same f0, no burst without a pitch line in the gaps >= 0.25 s. Atabaques' fast-falling hits have centroids of 1000-1500 Hz: atabaque strokes. A clip-by-clip check cannot prove a mute absent, but nothing in the material can be fitted.
- So **Mute is a labelled judgement** and there is **no Clack**. Mute: the stick strikes while the other hand holds the bell. At the hit the ring already on the bell is multiplied by kp = 0.3, every mode's decay rate by G = 12 + 12 min(f / 6 kHz, 1) (the highs die first) relaxing over tau = 1.5 s, the strike's spectrum loses 2 dB/oct above 1 kHz, the contact is duller (0.7 x the bandwidth) and its tick 0.7 x. T60 of the High's fundamental then is 0.08 s. These numbers are not measured from anything.

## Model (scripts/agogo_engine.py)
Per hit: NS = 24 sinusoid resonators per bell at measured ratios of the bell's f1 (Tune moves all), per-mode T60 (Decay scales), levels W (Tone tilts +-6 dB/oct), the stick: a contact pulse -> two one-pole low-passes (`fc`, Touch, velocity) -> modes with per-stroke per-bell gains W (referred to the contact's own gain at Touch 50, vel 0.8: `agogo_engine.contact_db`), the modes' tone swells in over `ta` (fitted to the recordings' 10 % -> -3 dB rise: 1.1 / 1.3 ms) while a band-passed noise tick (`nl nf nq nt`) starts at once; Mute damping as above. Open: kp = 1, G = 1 (a stick does not damp a ringing bell; a Mute hit after it does). Vary jitters every sinusoid's gain by 1.5 dB std at each hit. Doublets are two table entries. Tables `scripts/agogo_tables.json` come from `tests/fit.py` and `tests/calibrate.py` (peak per stroke).
Dropped from Bongo: the 125 Hz body (Shell), noise bands, mod-wheel Press, the Flam, 5 of 6 strokes. No mod-wheel mute: not trivial enough to be worth a parameter and a test.

## Traps hit
- `fit.py`'s first pitch reference was the strongest-level mode of the table, which was a 9 kHz partial extrapolated to t = 0: f1 (the bell's pitch) is the strongest ringing (T60 > 0.3 s) mode below 4.5 kHz.
- A free variable-projection refit moved a mode to a 50 ms transient at +14 dB: refits are bounded (f +-5 Hz, decay x1.6) after dropping T60 < 80 ms and anything 45 dB under the strongest of the cluster.
- The band-envelope regression's intercept over-read the highs (Low's centroid 4834 Hz against 1317): matching each partial's first-40-ms energy fixed it.
- The contact low-pass would not slow the attack with the modes' phases fixed; `ta` (tone swell) does, as in Bongo. The 10 % -> -3 dB rise and the blip (RMS of the first 1.1 ms over the first-10-ms peak) are fitted jointly: `nl` to the blip, `ta` to the rise.
- Measuring a hit hit a Hann window's zero edge: tests that look at the first 30 ms must start the note a few ms in (Touch test read 0.0 dB).
- Bongo's 0.3 s assumptions (0.5 s renders, 1.5 s hit spacing, 400 ms plots, `-100 dB after 300 ms`) all lengthened: a High rings 1.4 s (T60), so renders are 3 s per hit, plots 2 s.
