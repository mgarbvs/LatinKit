# CR78

A Max for Live instrument modelled on the **Roland CR-78** voice board (VG-11A): 14 voices on 15 MIDI notes, built from the circuits in the
June 1979 service notes rather than from samples. `m4l/CR78.amxd` (**keep the `m4l/` folder together**: `cr_midi.js` and `cr_view.js` sit beside it;
the `.maxpat` is the generated source). Generated by `scripts/build_maxpat.py` and `scripts/cr_engine.py`; never hand-edit the patch.

## Panel

Functional, no skeuomorphic look yet. Screenshots (Live): `docs/screens/panel_page{0..3}.png`.

A vertical tab on the left picks one of four pages; every voice keeps playing. Each voice is a strip: name, a **TRIG** button (guiro: **H** and **L**) and its dials.
Accent, Master and Drive on the right are always visible. Hover any control for what it does.

| Page | Voices and dials |
|---|---|
| DRUMS | Bass Drum, Snare (+ Noise), Rimshot, Clave: Tune, Decay, Level, Pan |
| LATIN | High Bongo, Low Bongo, Low Conga: Tune, Decay, Level, Pan |
| NOISE | Hi-Hat (Decay, shared **Tone**, Level, Pan), Cymbal and Maracas (Decay, Level, Pan), Metallic Beat (Tune, Decay, **Amount**, **On Hats**, Level, Pan) |
| PERC | Cowbell (Tune, Decay, Tone, Level, Pan), Tambourine (Decay, Tone, **Shake**, Level, Pan), Guiro (Rate, Tone, Decay, Level, Pan) |

Units: **Tune/Tone/Rate** in semitones (0 = factory value, +-12). **Decay** in octaves of time (0 = factory decay to 1/10 amplitude, +-2 = x4 / x0.25).
**Level** in volts peak-to-peak with the service notes' amplitude as default (0.40 Vpp = a 0.4 peak sample; Live's meter reads about 3 dB lower through its mixer). Pan is constant power; the real machine is mono, so every default is centre.
**Accent** (default 60 %): velocity 100 plays the factory level; 100 % makes level follow velocity linearly, 0 % ignores it. **Drive**: tanh saturation on the ringing oscillators (BD, SD tone, bongos, conga), default 0.
Output has a soft limiter that is linear below 0.8.

## Voice devices (one voice per instrument, for Drum Rack pads)

Thirteen more instruments, `m4l/CR78 <Name>.amxd` (keep `m4l/` together: `cr_voice_midi.js` and `cr_voice_view.js` sit beside them; `CR78.amxd` is unchanged):
**Kick, Snare, Rim, Clave, Bongo, Conga, Hat, Cymbal, Maracas, Metal** (Metallic Beat), **Cowbell, Tamb, Guiro**. Any MIDI note plays the voice; velocity is the accent; the guiro runs while its note is held.
Generated by `scripts/build_voices.py` (`--params` lists every parameter); each gen~ is `voice_code()` in `cr_engine.py`: only that voice's code, the same functions as the full device.
The look is Ableton's Drum Synth style, like Shaker (180 x 169): a dark display with an orange line drawing that flashes on every hit and names the last note, orange value boxes / menus, three dials below (Tune or Tone or Rate, Decay, Volume).

| Device | Dials | Display |
|---|---|---|
| Kick, Conga | Tune, Decay, Volume | Accent, Drive |
| Snare | Tune, Decay, Volume | Noise, Accent, Drive |
| Rim, Clave, Metal | Tune, Decay, Volume | Accent |
| Bongo | Tune, Decay (both drums), Volume | **Bongo** High / Low, Accent, Drive |
| Hat, Cymbal, Maracas | **Tone**, Decay, Volume | Accent |
| Cowbell | Tune, Decay, Volume | Tone, Accent |
| Tamb | Tone, Decay, Volume | **Shake** Off / On, Accent |
| Guiro | **Rate**, Decay, Volume | **Stroke** Short / Long, Tone, Accent |

Ranges and units are the full device's (Tune / Tone / Rate +-12 st, Decay +-2 oct, Noise 0-200 %, Accent 0-100 % at 60, Drive 0-100 % at 0). Differences from the full CR78:
- **Volume** is in dB (-36 .. +6, default 0) instead of Level in Vpp. 0 dB is the table amplitude, exactly what the full device plays at Level's default **before its pan law**: a voice device is 3 dB louder per channel than the full one at centre pan (both outputs carry the same signal). One soft limiter, as in the full device.
- No Pan (a Drum Rack chain pans), no Master. Drive exists only on the ringing oscillators (kick, snare, bongo, conga). Metal has no Amount / On Hats (they layer it on hats), Hat / Cymbal / Maracas each have the shared band-pass's **Tone** as a dial (on the full device it is one control for all three), and Snare's Noise and Tamb's Shake are as there.
- Bongo's gen~ holds both bongo resonators (Tune and Decay move both); the menu only chooses which one a note rings, so the other's tail is not cut. Guiro's holds both strokes the same way.
- With the same parameters and hits each device's output equals the full engine's mono sum sample for sample (`tests/test_voices.js`; only a rimshot hit above velocity ~110 differs, by the limiter, since the full device's pan law keeps it below the knee).
- The Metal drawing is a triangle (stand-in for a metallic percussion; the real voice is three oscillators), unheard and unseen in Live like the rest of the look.

## How the voices are built

- **BD, SD tone, HB, LB, LC** are *pinged ringing oscillators*: a pulse kicks a damped 2-pole rotation, so the frequency and the decay to 1/10 are exactly the table's. No pitch envelope.
- **Rimshot and clave** are the same resonator (the real ones are pinged LC tanks).
- **One noise source** feeds snare noise, hi-hat, cymbal, maracas and tambourine. Hi-hat, cymbal and maracas pass the same LC band-pass with three envelopes that sum (no choke); the band-pass is linear, so three parallel copies sound the same as the board's one summed VCA and can be panned apart.
- **Metallic Beat**: three free-running square oscillators (6170, 5620, 4080 Hz) -> slightly nonlinear VCA (50 ms linear ramp) -> tank. **On Hats** also fires it from hi-hat and cymbal notes, at Amount x its Level.
- **Cowbell**: two free-running, lopsided sine oscillators (800, 555 Hz) -> slightly nonlinear VCA (60 ms linear ramp) -> tank.
- **Tambourine**: noise x envelope -> tank. **Shake** adds a second kick 70 ms later.
- **Guiro**: a pulse train at 125 / 77 Hz that runs while the note is held; each pulse opens a ~9 ms sin³ grain of the shared noise (level random ±38 %) into a resonant high-pass (5.9 kHz, Q 10) and a 6 kHz low-pass. Fitted to Ableton's Core Library `Samples/One Shots/Drums/Wood/Guiro C78 *.aif` (grain width, level spread, spectrum within ~3 dB); the first version (pulses pinging a narrow tank) sounded nasal.

## Differences from the real CR-78 (and every unverified value)

- **Models of the circuits, not simulations.** No supply sag or component spread; every hit of a voice is identical (the noise and the free-running oscillator phases aside).
- **Fitted to recordings, not read from the board:** the schematic scan is illegible at the tanks, so these are fitted to Ableton's Core Library one-shots (`Samples/One Shots/Drums/*/* C78.aif`; `docs/fit_ab.wav` and `docs/guiro_ab.wav` alternate sample / ours, git-ignored). Pitch, decay and level stay the service-note table's; the samples' unit was tuned 3-7 % differently and several samples are cut short.
  - hi-hat / cymbal / maracas band-pass **8.7 kHz, Q 11** and tambourine **4.4 kHz, Q 17.5** (single band-pass, ~2 dB rms from the sample spectra);
  - cowbell: each oscillator lopsided (its own 2nd / 3rd harmonic), a quadratic VCA (sum / difference tones), tank **1.66 kHz, Q 1.7**: harmonics and intermodulation within ~5 dB;
  - Metallic Beat: quadratic VCA, tank **6.2 kHz, Q 5** (no Tone knob): line levels and difference tones within ~3 dB, the faint >9 kHz sum tones ~10 dB low;
  - cowbell and Metallic Beat envelopes are **linear ramps** (the samples' are, to within ~0.05), reaching 1/10 at the table's 60 / 50 ms;
  - snare noise: level and a 2 kHz (two-pole) high-pass with a 9 kHz roll-off, band levels within ~2 dB of the sample (`docs/snare_ab.wav`); it was ~20 dB too quiet;
  - guiro grain and filter (see above).
  The tonal voices (bass drum, rimshot, clave, bongos, conga) already match their samples' shape (one damped sine each); only the sampled unit's trims differ.
  The samples' cymbal and tambourine decay about twice as fast as the table's 350 / 220 ms; the table is kept.
- **Other assumptions:** the ringing oscillators have no pitch drop (none visible); the first peak is normalised so Level is the peak; the snare's tone and noise share the table's 60 ms (the service-note scope picture suggests the tone is shorter, ~20 ms; the Noise knob is at the scope's 0.4 V : 0.7 V balance); the snare noise has a 6 kHz pole (4.7 k || 5.6 nF);
  the tambourine's second kick (70 ms, 80 % strength) and the guiro's 20 ms minimum stroke, 1 ms attack / 4 ms release are mine; the guiro's slow ramp (R546/C531) is not modelled.
- **Levels** follow the table's Vpp as the peak of a hit (noise voices: the median hit). Hits sum linearly, so several at once pass 0.8 into the soft limiter.
- **Not modelled:** the rhythm patterns and sequencer, sound killer, the accent stage's exact curve (velocity replaces it), separate outputs, the VG-11 (older) board, the OP-103 "Add Voice" sliders as such (Shake / On Hats / the guiro notes stand in), analogue drift. Metallic Beat has one Tune for its three oscillators (the board has three trim pots).
- Hi-hat, cymbal and maracas never choke each other, as on the board; there is no hat/cymbal cancel button.
- The Tune / Decay units (semitones, octaves of time) are mine; the board has trim pots only.
- Not checked: how it sounds to a CR-78 owner, hover text inside Live, CPU. The look is a placeholder.
